Behind the matches

How scoring works & sources

Scores combine activity, enrichment, space, size, grooming, shedding, and experience with your selected coat and household preferences. Default weights are activity 25, enrichment 15, space 10, size 15, grooming 15, shedding 10, and experience 10. A coat preference adds weight 30; children and pets add 15 each when selected; quieter dogs, visitor sociability, and less drool add 10 each when selected. Your priority doubles its relevant base weights, then the score is normalized. Household and coat traits are authored teaching estimates, not safety guarantees or official breed ratings. Long days away without support cap scores at 55. Fluffy-only and shorter-coat-only choices filter the live shortlist and swipe deck; the full catalog remains available with mismatches labeled. Coat fullness 0 means sleek, 1 short/wiry, 2 full/feathered, and 3 very full/curly. Grooming can change appearance.

Numerical profiles are authored teaching estimates, not AKC ratings. General breed descriptions are informed by the American Kennel Club: Havanese, Whippet, Labrador Retriever, Standard Poodle, Border Collie, and Greyhound. Additional profiles: Samoyed, Aussiedoodle, Golden Retriever, Goldendoodle, Jack Russell Terrier, Beagle, Boxer, Dachshund, Chihuahua, Shih Tzu, Maltese, Bichon Frise, Pomeranian, Yorkshire Terrier, Miniature Schnauzer, Pembroke Welsh Corgi, Shiba Inu, Siberian Husky, Alaskan Malamute, German Shepherd, Australian Shepherd, Bernese Mountain Dog, Newfoundland, Great Pyrenees, Saint Bernard, Great Dane, Doberman Pinscher, Rottweiler, Dalmatian, Vizsla, Weimaraner, English Cocker Spaniel, English Springer Spaniel, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Papillon, Boston Terrier, French Bulldog, Bulldog, Pug, Miniature Poodle, Toy Poodle, Border Terrier, West Highland White Terrier, Scottish Terrier, Shetland Sheepdog. Coat and household traits are simplified teaching estimates. Terrier is represented by a Jack Russell Terrier; doodle size and coat traits vary. Reviewed October 20, 2026.

Discovery combines overall profile fit (85%) with learned preferences (15%). Likes favor similar coat appearance, size, and activity; passes favor different traits. Learning strengthens over the first three choices. Only dogs matching your coat filter enter the live shortlist and swipe deck. Each matching type appears at most once per round. Changing a filter preserves previous choices. Restart clears swipe learning but retains favorites.
